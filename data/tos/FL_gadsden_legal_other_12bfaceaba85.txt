Records Request Policy – Gadsden County Supervisor of Elections Skip to content Records Request Policy Thank you for your interest in obtaining a public record. The Gadsden County Supervisor of Elections responds to public records and voter data requests on a first-in, first out basis. This page explains how you can request copies of public records from the Supervisor of Elections Office. What Records Can I Request? Florida has a broad public records law. Most documents are open to inspection and copying. This includes most records related to elections and voting. We will do our best to provide or direct you to existing available records. However, we are not required to create new records. We are also not required to compile or customize data to fulfill your request. Some information is confidential or exempt from disclosure. If you request a record that contains confidential or exempt information, we will redact any confidential or exempt part. Confidential or exempt information includes: Voters’ driver’s license, ID card, or Social Security numbers Records for pre-registered voters Information related to where a voter registered Identifying information of persons with high-risk professions or victims of certain crimes Information related to critical infrastructure Records related to cybersecurity, network schematics, or hardware and software configurations Trade secrets that are held by an agency Voter signatures can only be viewed, not copied Vote-by-Mail ballot request information can only be given to certain people and organizations Poll workers’ contact information How Can I Request Records?

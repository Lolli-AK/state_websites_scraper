INFORMATION WE COLLECT Our website automatically collects certain technical information to help us understand how the site is used and to ensure it functions properly. This may include: The domain and IP address used to visit the site The date and time of your visit The pages you viewed This information is used in aggregate to improve the website’s content, performance, accessibility, and security. It does not identify you personally, and we do not share this data with outside organizations. We may also collect information that you voluntarily submit through online forms or services. This information is requested only when necessary to complete a specific transaction, similar to what would be required if you completed the same request in person. HOW WE USE THAT INFORMATION When you complete an online form or request, the information you submit may be used for purposes such as: Processing a service request Sending confirmations or follow-up communications Responding to employment applications Depending on the service, information you provide may include: Name Residential or mailing address Telephone number Email address Identification or application numbers PUBLIC RECORDS NOTICE Florida has a very broad public records law. As a result, any written communication created or received by the Orange County Supervisor of Elections Office will be made available to the public and media, upon request, unless otherwise exempt. Under Florida law, F.S. 668.6076, e-mail addresses are public records. If you do not want your e-mail address released in response to a public records request, do not send electronic mail to this office. Instead, contact our office by phone or in writing.

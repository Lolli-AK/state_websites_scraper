Legal Notices | Jefferson County Supervisor of Elections Skip to content Search Website Search Website Legal Notices Notice of Ineligibility A notice of ineligibility is an official notification sent to an individual informing them that they are not currently eligible to vote. This notice may be issued for several reasons, such as not meeting age or residency requirements, not being a registered voter, or being disqualified due to a felony conviction or lack of citizenship. Receiving a notice of ineligibility typically means the person will not be allowed to cast a ballot until the issue is resolved. View Voter Eligibility Notices Secret Link

Welcome to the Florida Department of State's Election Watch Website!
Florida polls close at 7:00 PM local time. However, since Florida
has two time zones (Central and Eastern), preliminary election night results will not be released until 8:00 p.m. Eastern Time.
Note: This site requires JavaScript to be enabled. For best viewing, use the latest version of your browser (e.g. Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, Apple Safari and Opera).
To check if you have the latest version or to upgrade your browser, go directly to the vendor's secure website.

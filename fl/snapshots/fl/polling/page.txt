Voter Information Lookup
Use the
Voter Information Look-up
to check your voter registration and party status.
It may take 1-2 business days (longer during book closing periods) for new registrations and updates entered into the Florida Voter Registration System to be available through the look-up.
If you cannot find your information, please contact your
county Supervisor of Elections
or call the Division of Elections’ Voter Assistance Hotline at  1.866.308.6739.
Florida Relay Service
(TDD or voice) is available to persons who are deaf, hard of hearing, deaf/blind, or speech disabled. Dial 7-1-1. For more information visit the
Florida Relay Service
.
